# Hi, I’m Kumori

I build accessible, human-approved creative technology for artists, craftspeople, musicians, independent shops, and people without expensive hardware or large AI budgets.

I’m building NANOKAT around a simple idea: useful tools should leave people in control of their work, their data, and the decisions that matter.

## What I’m working on

### NANOKAT

Creative websites, small-business tools, and a learning-and-making hub. The work connects practical client projects with tools for creating, reviewing, and sharing artifacts.

[Visit NANOKAT](https://nanokat.com)

### Solforge

A human-approved creation workflow: turn an idea into a structured plan, review a preview, and verify the resulting artifact. The public repositories document the published work; ongoing development continues separately.

- [Public Solforge project](https://github.com/nanokataclysm/solforge-public)
- [NANOKAT Forge competition project](https://github.com/nanokataclysm/nanokat-forge)

### Aetherkin

An experimental, local-first personal companion with explicit consent, scoped permissions, and memory that can be corrected or forgotten. It is an active private research project, not a generally available product.

- **[nk-cli](https://github.com/nanokataclysm/nk-cli)** — public-safe assistive utilities for boundaries, diagnostics, and dry-run cleanup planning.

- **[they-them](https://github.com/nanokataclysm/they-them)** — Linux VST3 and standalone vocal pitch and formant shifting audio effect.
- **[make-synth](https://github.com/nanokataclysm/make-synth)** — modular drone and noise synthesizer available as VST3/CLAP/AU and in the browser.

## Current focus

- Making local AI and hosted models useful without giving them unrestricted authority.
- Improving companion memory, evidence handling, and operator controls.
- Building creative tools for music, visual work, and independent businesses.
- Keeping setup instructions, tests, and project status honest about what works today.

## Principles

- Human approval before consequential actions
- Secure and recoverable systems
- Honest claims about what is shipped
- Accessible tools for people with limited hardware or API budgets
- Small, reviewable, reversible changes
